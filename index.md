## Anish Ahuja

I'm a first-year PhD student at New York University. I'm advised by [Thibault Dardinier](https://dardinier.me/) and a member of the [ACSys group](https://cs.nyu.edu/acsys/). Prior to my PhD, I worked on the Alexa team at Amazon.

I received my Bachelors in Computer Science and Math at UC Davis, where I was a member of [DavisPL](https://www.github.com/DavisPL), led by [Caleb Stanford](https://web.cs.ucdavis.edu/~cdstanford/).

I'm interested in programming languages, formal methods, and automated verification. In particular, my goal is to develop sound theoretical foundations for SMT solvers and verification languages to build user-friendly, reliable tools for correctness properties.
